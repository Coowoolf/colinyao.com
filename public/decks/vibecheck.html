PHYSICS · LATENCY07
OUR BRAINS HAVE A STRICT SPEED LIMIT

Waiting kills the conversation.

Our tolerance for delay is not fixed — it depends on who we think is on the other side.Most agents today live in the third box: usable, but that is no longer a conversation — it is command and response.
MONEY QUOTE · 02
If a human pauses, we assume they are thinking. If an agent pauses, we assume it is broken.
The same silence reads as thinking in a human — and as broken in an agent. That is the asymmetric penalty of latency.
PHYSICS · ATTENTION09
THE COCKTAIL PARTY PROBLEM

Noise doesn't kill the vibe. Distraction does.

Engineering has solved the first two boxes. The one that wrecks conversations is the third.
PHYSICS · ATTENTION10
WE CALL THIS

Selective Attention Locking.

DE-NOISE Remove what you don't want Subtract what you do not want. This path solved steady noise — AC, fans, machinery — and absorbs most sudden bursts.
ATTENTION LOCKING Decide who you listen to Lock onto your speaker in a room full of voices. That takes selective attention + context + source separation, not stronger de-noising.
A mature agent doesn't just de-noise. It must know who to listen to.

SURVIVAL · THE 3 LAWS15
THE 3 LAWS OF VOICE AGENT SURVIVAL

Three laws. Miss one and there is no vibe.

LAW 01Don't Mishear.Selective Attention Locking — lock onto your speaker in a room full of voices, instead of treating everything you hear as input.
LAW 02Don't Break.Observable Cascaded Architecture — every millisecond of latency and every failure point can be traced, swapped, and recovered.
LAW 03Don't Make People Wait.Real-time Network — push the response back into the range where the brain still treats it as conversation.
These three are not bonus points. They are the entry bar.Survive first, then talk experience. Break any one of the three and you fail the vibe check.
