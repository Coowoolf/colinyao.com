Scale, productivity and compliance pull against each other.

02 · FIVE FRICTIONS · WHERE IT BREAKS
01
Contact
Call blocking, refusals and dead numbers cut effective contact; a phone-only channel keeps losing reach.
02
Productivity
Agent training is slow and top performers are hard to clone; peak volume means hiring, and cost stops flexing.
03
Strategy
Ageing, amount, product and risk each need a different script and rhythm; rule-based policies are slow to change.
04
Compliance
Scripts, frequency, outsourcing and complaints all need managing; manual conversations rest on individual judgement.
05
Quality assurance
Sampling covers a fraction of calls, and post-hoc review cannot stop a risky script while it is being spoken.
These are structural problems — more headcount does not solve them.

REGULATORY SPEC · VIETNAM AND THE REGION
Regulation has already written the collections spec.
01 · THE SPEC · SBV CIRCULAR 18/2019/TT-NHNN
CIRCULAR · STATE BANK OF VIETNAM
Circular 18/2019/TT-NHNN
Amends Circular 43/2016, Point dd, Clause 2, Article 7 · effective 01.01.2020
01 · FREQUENCY
≤ 5 reminders per day
Debt reminder contact is capped at five times a day.
02 · TIME WINDOW
07:00–21:00 only
Contact only inside the window agreed in the loan contract.
03 · WHO
No third-party contact
No reminders or collection messages to organisations or people with no obligation to repay, unless a regulator requires it.
04 · MEANS
Lawful measures only
Measures must be lawful, and must exclude threatening the customer.
WHY IT MATTERS
A spec only systems can enforce at scale.
Frequency caps, time windows, third-party limits and language limits are parameters, not guidance.
A system can hold them on every call and log the evidence. A person cannot hold them on every call, every day, at volume.
Every contact then answers three questions: why this customer, what strategy, and was it compliant.

8/15
TRENDS · FIVE DIRECTIONS
Five directions the industry is already moving in.
01 · FIVE TRENDS · COMPLIANCE · STANDARDISATION · INTELLIGENCE · NEGOTIATION · CONTROL
COMPLIANCE-FIRST
Compliance first
The bar moves from outcome only to process and outcome. Institutions must show that every action met regulation, internal policy and customer-protection rules.
STANDARDISATION
Standardisation
Process, language, contact frequency, records and quality review become standard, cutting person-to-person variance and outsourcing risk.
INTELLIGENCE
Intelligence
AI is used for segmentation, contact timing, voice outreach, intent detection, real-time quality review, call summaries and strategy tuning.
NEGOTIATION
Negotiation
The work moves from reminding to assessing capacity to repay, discussing instalment options, resolving disputes and repairing the relationship.
INTERNAL CONTROL
Internal control
Lenders carry more responsibility for third-party agencies, and need systems to push strategy, monitor process, review quality and audit.
AI in collections is not an isolated tool — it is the natural next step.
